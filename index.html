What we do

From the first sketch to the handover of the building — one office, one drawing set, one person answering the phone.

  1. Sheet A-01

    Architecture

    Full commission, from feasibility and concept through to permit — public buildings, health, mixed-use and housing.

  2. Sheet A-02

    Health & public

    Hospitals, clinics and civic buildings, planned around daylight, movement, and the equipment that cannot be moved.

  3. Sheet A-03

    Interior architecture

    Fit-out from the first plan to the last joinery drawing: clinics, apartments, workplaces. Materials, light, detail.

  4. Sheet A-04

    Mixed-use & retail

    Towers, podiums and the public ground beneath them — including facade strategy, planting and lighting.

  5. Sheet A-05

    Delivery on site

    Execution drawings, contractor coordination and site supervision, through to handover of the finished building.
